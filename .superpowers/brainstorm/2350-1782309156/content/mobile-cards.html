A — Single Column Stack (Recommended)

All cards stack vertically, full width. Clean and readable. Each card gets full attention before scrolling to the next.

B — 2-Column on Mobile

Two cards per row, third spans full width. More compact, shows more above the fold. Works well on larger phones (375px+).

C — Horizontal Scroll

Cards scroll horizontally like a carousel. Space-efficient and engaging, but users might miss cards. Works better on touch devices.
